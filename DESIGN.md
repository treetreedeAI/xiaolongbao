# 一起做小籠包

Surface: experience. Change mode: extend the user-supplied artwork. Color strategy: incumbent.

Composition: one softly lit plush cooking stage, one visible action, and a quiet nine-dot progress row; a child should always know what to tap next.

Use cream backgrounds, pale wheat-beige objects, dark warm-brown type, and muted forest-green primary actions. Preserve the original character, emblem, proportion and material; do not generate replacement characters. Every title and instruction is real HTML. Mobile is portrait; tablet reflows in either orientation; wide screens have a landscape stage and comfortably reachable controls. Never stretch the artwork.

Motion explains each small action; reduced-motion uses short fades. No audio, account, score, purchases, or unrelated features. Pause active time when the page is hidden or a confirmation dialog is open.
